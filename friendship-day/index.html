01
The Letter

I'm not usually the kind of person who builds a whole website about someone. You've been the exception to that — the same way you've been the exception to most of my rules.

Some friendships you have to work at. Ours never asked me to perform, or explain, or be a tidier version of myself. You just decided I was worth keeping, quietly, and then kept me.

So this is the smallest way I know to say the loud thing out loud: thank you for being easy to know and impossible to forget.

— Kalyan
