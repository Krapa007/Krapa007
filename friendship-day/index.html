Chapter 01 · The distance

March

Where it started — quietly, and without a clue what it would become.

We met back in March and stayed politely, pleasantly at arm's length for months — friends in the loosest sense of the word. Nothing in it hinted at any of this.

Chapter 02 · The turn

This week

When it changed — all at once, faster than either of us expected.

Then, in the space of a few days, the distance just started closing — fast, the way it does maybe once or twice in a whole life. Suddenly there was nowhere left to be polite about.

Chapter 03 · The night

Tonight.

2 August 2026 · past midnight

When it disappeared completely.

We talked all the way down to the bottom of things — the kind of conversation you can't plan and can't fake. Nothing performed, nothing held back, just the two of us actually meeting each other for the first time. I don't have a tidier word for what happened in that hour than magic, so I'm not going to look for one.
