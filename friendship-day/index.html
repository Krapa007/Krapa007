01
The Letter

I'm not usually the kind of person who builds a whole website about someone. But it's the middle of the night, we've only really known each other properly for about a week, and I already can't picture the version of my life that doesn't have you in it.

We met back in March and stayed politely, pleasantly at arm's length for months — friends in the loosest sense of the word. Then this past week the distance just started closing, fast, and tonight it disappeared completely.

So this is the smallest way I know to say the loud thing out loud: thank you for tonight, and for being the rare kind of person who's easy to know and impossible to forget.

— Kalyan

02 — The One I Keep
2 August 2026 · past midnight
Tonight we talked all the way down to the bottom of things — and something quietly rearranged itself.

We've technically been friends since March, but for months we were just — around each other. Polite. Pleasant. At arm's length. It was only this past week that the distance started closing, fast, the way it does maybe once or twice in a whole life.

And then tonight, the 2nd of August, we had the kind of conversation you can't plan and can't fake — nothing performed, nothing held back, just the two of us actually meeting each other for the first time. I don't have a tidier word for what happened in that hour than magic, so I'm not going to look for one.

I'm writing it down while it's still warm, so neither of us ever gets to call it small.
